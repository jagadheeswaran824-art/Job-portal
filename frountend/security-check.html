Candidate Protection Guide

Essential rules to safeguard your career journey, personal documents, and financial safety.

1. Never Pay Upfront Fees

Legitimate employers never charge candidates for application processing, background checks, training kits, or mandatory equipment purchases.

2. Protect Banking & OTPs

Never share net banking credentials, OTP codes, credit card details, or UPI PINs during an interview or hiring process.

3. Verify Official Domains

Cross-check recruiter email addresses against official corporate websites. Watch out for free webmail accounts claiming to represent Fortune 500s.

4. Beware Direct Executable Downloads

Never download or open .exe, .bat, or script files masquerading as job application forms or assessment tests.

5. Interview via Verified Channels

Be cautious if interviews take place exclusively over anonymous messaging apps (Telegram / WhatsApp) without a corporate email or video call.

6. Realistic Compensation

Offers advertising astronomical salaries for entry-level tasks with zero qualifications are almost always deceptive schemes.
